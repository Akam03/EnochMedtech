Our Expertise

Focused on the future of cardiac intervention

We specialize in the sales and marketing of advanced minimally invasive coronary and structural heart devices, working across the evolving cardiac healthcare ecosystem.

Coronary Intervention

Advanced technologies supporting the treatment of coronary artery disease and complex coronary lesions.

  • Drug Eluting Stents (DES)
  • Drug Coated Balloons (DCB)
  • PTCA Balloon & CTO Balloon
  • PTCA Guide Wires

Structural Heart

Technology supporting minimally invasive treatment approaches for structural heart conditions.

  • Aortic Valve Systems (TAVR)
  • Balloon Mitral Valvuloplasty (BMV)
  • ASD & VSD Closure Devices

Coronary Imaging

Advanced imaging technologies that help clinicians visualize and assess coronary anatomy.

  • IVUS (Intravascular Ultrasound)
  • OCT (Optical Coherence Tomography)

Calcium Modification, Cath Lab Support & Vascular Accessories

Calcium modification devices and a comprehensive range of cath lab accessories and procedural support products designed to complement interventional procedures.

  • Cutting & Scoring Balloons
  • IVL
  • Orbital Atherectomy
  • Cath Lab Accessories
  • Vascular Accessories
